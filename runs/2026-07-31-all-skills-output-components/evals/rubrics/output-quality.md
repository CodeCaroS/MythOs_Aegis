# Output Contract Rubric

Score every dimension from 0 to 1. The weights are fixed before candidate work.

- Outcome usability (0.20): the result, verdict, or requested artifact appears before process narration.
- Evidence fidelity (0.20): observed facts, sources, assumptions, and unknowns are distinguishable.
- Actionability (0.15): findings, decisions, or plans expose the smallest useful next action.
- Verification honesty (0.15): checks and results are concrete; unrun or incomplete checks are not presented as passed.
- Domain fit (0.15): the output preserves the skill's specialized fields and safety boundaries.
- Proportionality (0.10): empty sections and irrelevant ceremony are omitted; depth follows impact.
- Reuse (0.05): the skill composes a named shared profile instead of cloning generic response scaffolding.

Hard pass requires:

- no fabricated evidence, completion, source, or test result;
- no safety, authorization, privacy, or destructive-action regression;
- every profile-required field is either rendered or explicitly marked not applicable;
- artifact-only tasks do not wrap the requested artifact in irrelevant report sections.

Composite pass threshold: 0.80. Candidate acceptance requires at least +0.01
over its frozen baseline and no critical or domain-specific regression.
